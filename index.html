Stacking-Based ML for Cardiovascular Disease

Cardiovascular disease is the leading global cause of mortality. Early identification through routine clinical indicators allows timely preventative treatments.

Methodology

  • Combined multiple base models (Random Forest, XGBoost, SVM) into a unified ensemble.
  • Employed Logistic Regression as the meta-learner to blend predictions from base classifiers.
  • Achieved significant reduction in classification variance and validated results through a published research paper.
